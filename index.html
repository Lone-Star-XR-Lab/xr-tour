The pilot-program loop

Throw spaghetti. Keep what sticks.

Not every app or idea works, and that is useful information. The lab improves by testing, observing, gathering feedback, and adjusting.

Try

Choose an experience or teaching idea worth exploring.

Observe

See how students and faculty actually use it.

Learn

Identify what worked, what failed, and what created value.

Improve

Refine the process, keep the useful pieces, and try again.

15-second answer

“We’re a pilot program for educational XR. We test apps and teaching ideas, figure out what works, support faculty, and give students opportunities to build durable skills.”

  1. Welcome: XR and mixed reality for education.
  2. Pilot program: We constantly test and improve.
  3. What we use: Mostly existing, accessible, and free experiences.
  4. Who we support: Students, faculty, and many departments.
  5. Why it matters: Communication, teamwork, critical thinking, problem solving, and adaptability.
  6. The goal: Keep the equipment in use instead of collecting dust.
